The Setup

Zeal Consumer Ventures is a three-year-old internal venture unit of Bharat Industrial Group — a ₹9,000 Cr conglomerate built on cement, steel tubes and agri-inputs. Three years ago the board made a bet: we are too dependent on cyclical B2B businesses; build us a consumer engine. Zeal's research phase is complete. Five market opportunities are on the table. The board meets in 72 hours.

"Convert what we already own — reach, raw material, capacity, and trust in Tier 2/3 India — into a consumer business. ₹500 Cr. 36 months. One shot."

Your team is Zeal's leadership. Your output is a Board Memo the Chairman can act on. Every recommendation must survive his three questions: Why this? Why now? Why us?
